Cookies & browser storage

Updated November 18, 2026

What the storefront uses

Our application code does not set browser cookies. It uses local storage and session storage for the bag, welcome offer and privacy settings. Hosting, authentication and security services may set their own necessary cookies or process connection information. This page describes the storage controlled by this storefront.

Optional fonts

Google Fonts is the only optional external resource controlled by this storefront. It is off until enabled. When enabled, your browser requests font files from Google and sends the technical information required to make that request. Turning it off removes the font link and stops future requests from this control. It cannot recall information already transmitted or remove files from your browser cache.

Your controls


Core storage is used when you choose to build a bag or enter an email. You can clear it here or block storage in your browser. Blocking it may prevent selections from surviving a refresh. These controls affect this device and browser. They do not delete sent texts, order records, bank transactions or platform authentication cookies.

Opt-out signals and tracking

The current storefront performs no sale of personal data, targeted advertising or significant-decision profiling. When a Global Privacy Control signal is detected, optional fonts stay off and the signal is shown in Privacy settings. We will update these controls and notices before adding optional tracking.
