Cash payment, pricing & tax

Updated November 18, 2026

Current prices

Each listed print costs $4. Any 10 pieces, mixed across products and available colors, cost $30. Extra singles are $4 each. WELCOME20 reduces the merchandise subtotal by 20% when valid for the order. The discount is applied before tax.

Tax on New Jersey orders

These taxable New Jersey orders include sales tax at 6.625%, rounded to the nearest cent at the order level. The tax base is the discounted merchandise subtotal plus taxable delivery charges if any. Cash payment does not make a taxable sale exempt. This checkout currently supports New Jersey local orders.

Cash on delivery

Payment is collected in cash at the confirmed local handoff. No bank details are requested. Review the itemized total before requesting an order and confirm the final total and arrangements with the store. We provide an order record showing the merchandise, discount, tax and cash due.

Order inbox

Order notifications are routed to anvith206@gmail.com through the configured email service after inbox activation. The request is also saved for the store. An email provider acceptance message does not guarantee inbox delivery or final order acceptance. We confirm availability and the handoff separately.

Refunds

Eligible refunds are monetary, generally returned in cash by an agreed arrangement. A store-credit offer does not replace a mandatory monetary refund. See the 30-day Returns policy.
