Age suitability & adult buyers

Updated November 18, 2026

Intended users and buyers

Products are intended for people aged 13 and older. Buyers must be adults aged 18 or older. Parents and guardians may purchase for teens aged 13–17. The entry checkbox is a self-declaration, not proof of identity or a safety certification.

Product use

Keep products and broken pieces away from younger children and pets. Small parts, breakage or sharp edges can present hazards. Stop using a damaged product. Use spinning items on a stable surface away from faces, fragile objects and moving machinery. Confirm the actual design, material and dimensions before use.

Adult-focused marketing

Our store addresses adult buyers and parents. “Get your teen a desk favorite” refers to users aged 13+. We do not make therapeutic, anxiety-treatment or other medical claims about fidget products.

Safety review

The manufacturer must assess the actual product, foreseeable use, construction, marketing, labeling and applicable standards. An age label alone does not establish compliance or remove applicable testing, labeling, certification or reporting obligations.

Privacy

The ordering forms are for adult buyers. We do not ask for a child’s name, birth date, school or other child profile. A parent or guardian can contact Customer Service to report information submitted by a child.
