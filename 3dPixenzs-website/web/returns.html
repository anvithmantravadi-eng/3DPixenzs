CUSTOMER POLICIES

30-day returns, refunds & cancellations

Request a return within 30 days of receiving an unused, undamaged standard product, including discounted purchases. Email anvith206@gmail.com with your order reference or other reasonable proof of purchase.

Local returns

Contact us before returning anything. We arrange a confirmed local return handoff; do not use an unconfirmed address. No restocking fee applies. A change-of-mind return does not include any separately paid delivery fee, where permitted.

Damaged, defective or wrong items

Describe the issue and include photos when available. We will arrange an appropriate repair, replacement or refund and cover necessary return costs when the problem is ours. This policy does not limit statutory remedies.

Refund amount and method

Approved refunds reflect the amount actually paid for returned products, including refundable tax. Bundle discounts are allocated proportionally. We issue monetary refunds to the original payer in cash at an agreed handoff within five business days after receipt and inspection, unless a shorter legal deadline applies. Mandatory refunds are not limited to store credit.

Cancellations

Request cancellation before handoff. We confirm the status; no cash payment is collected online. If shipping is enabled later, shipping terms and applicable cancellation rights will be disclosed before acceptance.

Email anvith206@gmail.com. See Order issues for complaint handling.
