Parcel shipping is off. We accept New Jersey local handoff requests, subject to availability and service-area confirmation. The adult buyer pays the confirmed total in cash when receiving the order. The total includes 6.625% New Jersey sales tax on these taxable purchases. There is no online payment or Zelle request in the current checkout.
