Privacy notice

Contact and scope

This notice covers the storefront, adult ordering forms and owner-only Admin. Contact anvith206@gmail.com or (908) 415-4730 for privacy questions. The seller’s legal business name and mailing address must be supplied before public launch.

Adult order information

We collect the adult buyer’s name, email, phone, New Jersey town, chosen products, colors, quantities, optional handoff details and notes, along with the age and policy declarations. We use this information to review the request, confirm local arrangements, fulfill it, provide support, record cash collection and meet applicable tax and legal obligations. Please do not include a child’s personal information or bank credentials.

How we protect order information

3dPixenzs stores order records on its hosting service. The storefront and Admin use HTTPS, and viewing saved customer names, order details and receipts in Admin requires the store owner’s authorized sign-in. We use this information to fulfill orders, provide support and keep business records. Receipts show the buyer’s name and purchase details. They omit phone numbers and private order notes.

When order notifications are enabled, our email provider may process order and contact details to deliver them to the store inbox. Our hosting and security providers may also process information needed to operate the site. We do not sell customer information.

Browser storage

The bag and coupon use local storage. The welcome email and adult-entry acknowledgment use session storage. A pending order reference and a hash of the request are stored temporarily to avoid duplicates on retry. Privacy settings include the notice acknowledgment and optional-font preference. See Cookies & storage for controls. The welcome form reveals a code on the page and does not create a marketing subscription.

Optional services

Optional Google Fonts loads only after you enable it. The current storefront has no advertising pixels or visitor-analytics tags. We do not sell personal data or use targeted advertising or significant-decision profiling in this implementation.

Retention and requests

Order, tax, payment and support records are retained as needed for fulfillment, legal recordkeeping and disputes. Information no longer needed is deleted unless retention is legally required or permitted. Use Privacy requests for access, correction, deletion, copy, opt-out or appeal requests where applicable. We may retain records required by law when responding to deletion requests.

Children

The forms and checkout are for adults aged 18 or older. We do not knowingly collect personal information online from children under 13. A parent or guardian may contact us if a child supplied information. The age checkbox is a self-declaration and does not establish identity.

Policy changes

We update the date and provide notice of material changes before applying new practices where required. New uses requiring consent will be presented before they start.
