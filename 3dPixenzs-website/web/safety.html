CUSTOMER POLICIES

Product care & safety

Updated November 18, 2026

Intended use

This catalog offers Spiderweb Fidgets, Spinning Tops and Spiral Spinners for adult use. Keep small pieces away from young children. Use spinning objects on a stable surface away from faces and fragile items. Confirm material and suitability before use. Use each item according to its listing and packaging. Unless specifically validated and labeled for that use, keep prints away from food and drink, flames, hot surfaces and high heat. Avoid dishwashers and microwaves.

Handling and fit

Keep small parts and broken pieces away from young children and pets. Stop using an item if it cracks, warps or develops sharp edges. Clean with a soft dry cloth or as directed on the product label. Check dimensions, fit and stability before use.

Custom design limits

We accept projects within our verified materials and manufacturing capabilities. We decline designs intended for weapons, medical use, electrical safety, structural loads, food contact or children’s products under this store scope.

Report a safety concern

Stop using the product and contact (908) 415-4730 by text with the product name, order number and a description. We will assess the report and meet any applicable reporting, notice or recall obligations.
