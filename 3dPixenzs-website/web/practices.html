STORE INFORMATION

How we run the store

Updated November 18, 2026

Product selection and variations

We offer the products and color choices shown in the catalog. Descriptions identify intended use and age suitability. We confirm material, dimensions, color availability and any material change before fulfillment.

Pricing and costs

Prices and discounts are shown before checkout. The store evaluates filament, print time, energy, failed prints, labor, packaging, fulfillment and other costs when setting prices. Admin includes a calculator for actual operating inputs.

Safety and quality

The store reviews small parts, edges, breakage, foreseeable use and applicable product requirements. Production checks include model accuracy, color, surface condition and quantity. We do not claim that website policies alone establish product compliance.

Design ownership

Selling a print requires the relevant commercial rights or another lawful basis. Design permissions, attributions and licenses must match the model and commercial use. A physical purchase does not automatically transfer digital files or reproduction rights.

Business registration, tax and records

The seller must complete applicable registration, tax collection and recordkeeping requirements and assess insurance needs. These operational steps are tracked in the Admin readiness checklist. Their presence in the checklist does not mean they have been completed.

Ordering and local fulfillment

Adult buyers review itemized prices, colors, New Jersey tax and cash due. Parcel shipping is currently disabled. Production capacity, packaging and local handoff arrangements are confirmed after review.

Returns and customer support

Our standard return window is 30 days after receipt for unused, undamaged products, subject to the Returns policy and preserved consumer rights. Order problems can be raised through Customer Service and the case-resolution process.

Marketing, reviews and analytics

Marketing is directed to adults and parents buying for users aged 13+. Product visualizations are identified. Reviews and performance claims must be genuine and supportable. Current Admin metrics are based on saved orders, statuses and recorded cash, not invented visitor counts. Optional analytics or advertising trackers require updated notices and controls before use.

Production and growth

Admin provides order stages, planned and actual print time, a handoff schedule, inventory and completion checklists. Capacity decisions use the store’s real records and available printer time.
