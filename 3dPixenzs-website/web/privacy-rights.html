New Jersey rights where applicable

The New Jersey Data Privacy Law generally covers controllers targeting New Jersey residents that process at least 100,000 consumers’ data in a calendar year, excluding data used solely to complete a payment, or at least 25,000 consumers’ data while benefiting from its sale. Legal exceptions apply. The store’s actual processing volume has not been established. We will handle requests under the laws that apply and consider other reasonable privacy requests.

Response, verification and appeals

Where the New Jersey law applies, we respond to a verified request within 45 days and explain any permitted extension of up to 45 additional days within the initial period. We may ask for information reasonably needed to verify access, correction, deletion or copy requests. We do not require a new account. If we decline, we explain why and how to appeal. Send an appeal through the same contact channel. Where required, we provide a written appeal decision within 45 days. You may also contact New Jersey Consumer Affairs.

Agents, opt-outs and equal treatment

An authorized agent can submit an opt-out request with information sufficient to establish authority as required by law. The store currently has no sale, targeted advertising or significant-decision profiling to opt out of. We honor applicable universal opt-out signals and do not penalize customers for exercising privacy rights. We retain records when required or permitted by law, explaining relevant limits to deletion.
