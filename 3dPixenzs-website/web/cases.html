Order issues & case resolution

Updated November 18, 2026

Open a case

For an order issue, cancellation, missing item, defect, damaged item or return, email anvith206@gmail.com or contact (908) 415-4730. Include your order reference and a short explanation. Photos help where available. Avoid sending bank credentials or unnecessary personal information.

How we review

We locate the request, review the product and handoff record and ask for relevant details if needed. We explain the proposed next step and any return instructions. We do not promise a fixed response time until staffing and opening hours are confirmed.

Available remedies

The appropriate remedy may include correction, replacement, repair, cancellation or refund, depending on the issue and applicable law. The standard unused-item return window is 30 days after receipt. This voluntary window does not shorten statutory remedies for defective or misdescribed goods.

Escalation

If a proposed resolution does not address your issue, reply to the same email and ask for an owner review. These procedures do not remove rights to contact a regulator or use remedies available under applicable law.
